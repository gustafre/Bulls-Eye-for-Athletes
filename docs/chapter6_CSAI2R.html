7.1 Competitive State Anxiety Inventory-2 Revised (CSAI-2R) and BEA validity analysis

Competitive State Anxiety Inventory-2 Revised (CSAI-2R) is used as part of the validity investigation of BEA. CSAI-2R consists of 17 items and three sub-scales divided into the following according to Cox et al. (2003) and Lundqvist & Hassmén (2005): Cognitive anxiety sub-scale (items 7, 10, 13, 16, 22); Somatic anxiety sub-scale (items 5, 8, 11, 17, 20, 23, 26); Self-confidence sub-scale (items 9, 15, 18, 24, 27). Note that the original item numbers from CSAI-2 are used. These three sub-scales of CSAI-2R will be used and analyzed separate with Rasch analysis. Validity analyses by correlations of thetas between BEA and each respective CSAI-2R sub-scale will then be undertaken. Lastly, partial correlations were conducted to examine the association between BEA and CSAI-2R sub-scales, while controlling for the shared variance among the sub-scales. Note that CSAI-2R was not completed by the participants from the intervention study (Hockeystudien), therefore more missing values than in the BEA Rasch analysis are to be expected.

## Competitive State Anxiety Inventory-2 Revised (CSAI-2R) and BEA validity analysis
Competitive State Anxiety Inventory-2 Revised (CSAI-2R) is used as part of the validity investigation of BEA. CSAI-2R consists of 17 items and three sub-scales divided into the following according to Cox et al. (2003) and Lundqvist & Hassmén (2005): Cognitive anxiety sub-scale (items 7, 10, 13, 16, 22); Somatic anxiety sub-scale (items 5, 8, 11, 17, 20, 23, 26); Self-confidence sub-scale (items 9, 15, 18, 24, 27). Note that the original item numbers from CSAI-2 are used. These three sub-scales of CSAI-2R will be used and analyzed separate with Rasch analysis. Validity analyses by correlations of thetas between BEA and each respective CSAI-2R sub-scale will then be undertaken. Lastly, partial correlations were conducted to examine the association between BEA and CSAI-2R sub-scales, while controlling for the shared variance among the sub-scales. Note that CSAI-2R was not completed by the participants from the intervention study (Hockeystudien), therefore more missing values than in the BEA Rasch analysis are to be expected.
